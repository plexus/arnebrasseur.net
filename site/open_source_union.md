# Independent Open Source Developers Union

A interest group for and by open source developers, united by a desire to
develop a strong digital commons.

A militant entity that uses union tactics (like strikes or collective
withholding) to protect the digital commons.

Use collective bargaining power by witholding support to big corporates who
don't proportionally donate back.

Lobby for government support, for open standards, level playing field, resist
corporate capture.

Advise on governance models.

Do fund raising, distribute grants.

Provide non-corporate infrastructure.

- Encourage/coerce companies to allow people to contribute back upstream
- Encourage/coerce companies to be transparent about the open source they use
- Get constractors to include open source clauses in their contracts

If all fortune 500 companies gave 0.1% of their revenue to open source, we'd
have a budget in the single digit billions.

Look into
- Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC)
- Open Source Collective (OSC)
- Sovereign Tech Fund (STF) 
- FSFE
