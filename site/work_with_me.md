My technical experience is both broad — I am a quick learner and have worked
with many technologies over the years — but it is also deep. I tend to build up
a profound and layered understanding of the core technologies I work with. These
subject areas in particular stand out:

- **UNIX and Linux**: ~3 decades of experience. Desktop and server, system
  administration, configuring and maintaining production systems, diagnosing
  issues. I know these systems and their tools inside and out.
- **Web Technologies and Standards**: ~2 decades of experience. From backend to
  frontend, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, HTTP-based APIs, REST and Hypermedia. Security,
  vulnerabilities, identity and auth. Whether it's URL, HTTP, or OAuth, I've
  read and often implemented the actual specs. The web platform is my home.
- **Clojure/Functional Programming/JVM**: ~13 years of experience. Clojure is what
  allowed me to ship on spec, in time, and on budget, time and time again; while
  maintaining a high bar for quality and maintainability. I know the ecosystem
  inside and out, and my libraries and tools have been widely adopted. Clojure
  also deepened my knowledge of the Java Virtual Machine, its capabilities as
  well as the things to watch out for in real-world deployments.

However, in the last 8 years I have rarely worked just as an Individual
Contributor. Instead my role was often that of a technological maestro, working
closely with stakeholders — to work out an implementation roadmap — and working
closely with individuals and teams around architecture, implementation,
deployment, and change management. In the process I discovered I have a number
of non-technical skills that can be somewhat rare in this industry.

- **I am a exceptional communicator**. I speak and write in a way that is clear and
  engaging, and can write both for a technical or a layperson audience. I
  understand the importance of communicating proactively.
- **I am a good listener**. I pay attention, and am good at understanding what
  people want to say, even if they're not saying it very clearly. Or I'll know
  what to ask, and when, to fill in my understanding.
- **I am dependable and have integrity**. I am true in my speech and in my
  actions. I am transparent whenever possible. I prefer to build up relations
  based on mutual trust.

I am looking for a place where I can spend the next five to ten years, at a
position that is both technical and strategic. Anything from Staff Engineer to
VP of Engineering.

My sweet spot is one where I can work with dev teams at a high level, and have
the leverage to make sure that we are building the right thing, in the right
way, by building bridges and aligning expectations, both with the people funding
and driving a project, and with the people ultimately using or being impacted by
the resulting software system.

- **Developing digital strategy** — Your organisation has certain goals and
  aspirations, but needs someone to help ground them and turn them into reality.
