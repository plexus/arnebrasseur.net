Hello there dear visitor. I'm not always going to greet you formally like this but since it's the first time I'm writing in this space, it does seem appropriate to do so.  
Nico just gave me a call about the flyer/poster design of the [hip-hop festival](http://www.flowability.com) we're organising. Apparently our headliner doesn't want to be the headliner. We have to put them on the bottom of the flyer/poster as 'special guests', plus they'll only be performing for 30 minutes. I don't mind too much, we still have five of the best Belgian hip-hop bands on the line-up. Now if only someone would write my thesis for me so I could concentrate on these important things.
