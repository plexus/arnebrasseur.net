_This was published on a personal blog I ran during the nillies, it's included here as a historical archive. Links and images may be broken, it's been a while._

So I've been going hard on the chinese lately, I'll be doing both oral and written exams on it soon so... Not that I have any worries about them. In fact the mood is surprisingly laid-back. I'm graduating this year so the uni exam drill isn't new for me anyway, still it's different each time. Normally I skip most of my classes and just study at home when the time comes. This year it's different in that I can't skip my chinese classes, (listening input is too important) so I can't do the "do what I want when I want to do it"-routine. Also it's been a long and cold winter, which I hate, and which has dragged me down a bit postponing the studying possibly a bit too long. Since yesterday the sun really started shining, so naturally I'll enjoy the weather first.

In a few weeks I'll definitly be doing many hours of studying a day, just not now. On wednesday the chinese department is giving a party, and everyone from the first year of modern chinese will perform a song. [Here](http://site332.mysite4now.com/plexus/modernchinees.be/yongbaomingtian.wma "Embracing tomorrow") you can listen to someones karaoke perfomance of the song. It's about the olympic games in Beijing 2008. The singing is getting better after two rehearsals, now we need to work on the choreographie. Tonight I'll be sound engineering on [Cheezy](http://www.cheezy.be) our weekly show on Urgent.fm, and after that there are bands performing in two different places in Gent. The one band I really like to see is [Grant Moff Tarkin](http://grantmofftarkin.be/ "MegaloMoffrica's number onest hit makers"). Their lead singer has a great voice and great looks, a killer combo IMHO. I hope I'll be in time.
