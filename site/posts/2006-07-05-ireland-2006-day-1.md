_This was published on a personal blog I ran during the nillies, it's included here as a historical archive. Links and images may be broken, it's been a while._

I'm in a youth hostel in Dublin where Internet costs 1€ for 15 bloody minutes, so a short message today. I'm by myself today, but tomorrow M arrives. We'll stay in Dublin for another day and then start hitchhiking to the EJC in Millstreet. I spent too much today, but I have found what I was looking for, a rain poncho, camping gaz and a map of Ireland. Dublin has a really nice vibe, I could get quite comfortable here.

I met a French juggler who showed me this hostel, which seems to be good value for money. This evening I'll be watching the World Cup in Temple Bar (famous street) with him, some other French guys and a Portuguese. Went to Trinity College but refused to pay 8€ to see the book of Kells. Just had a nice chat with some other Flemish guys. I bought a second hand softcover fantasy novel which I'll be enjoying the following days. Hopefully it's nice and I can swap it at the convention for something else. So now I'm going to enjoy a can of Stella (Guinness is for later) and my newly acquired book.
