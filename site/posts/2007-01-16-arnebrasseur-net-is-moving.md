_This was published on a personal blog I ran during the nillies, it's included here as a historical archive. Links and images may be broken, it's been a while._

During the week this site will move to a new provider. Doring this transition the site might be temporarily unavailable.

I'm moving from Webhost4life to Dreamhost for a number of reasons :

  * Apache, so finally mod_rewrite, hence clean URL's
  * Linux based with SSH access, anything a nerd could wish for
  * Support for Ruby/Rails
  * Create your own subversion repositories

Webhost4life appears to be very cheap, but in reality you often pay extra. For instance, I wanted to add a second domain (flowability.com) to my account : pay up. It was even registered by the same company, Webhost4life, for which I obviously had paid. Would you like to have e-mail on said domain (my demands tend to be fairly exotic), then it will also cost extra. The final drop was when I wanted to put an rdf file (FOAF). The server kept giving me a 404, even tough the file was clearly present. I opened a support ticket and swiftly came the answer : "No MIME type has been configurder for the rdf file type, that's why IIS is giving you a 404. To add MIME types, you need a more expensive hosting plan." 

The site [Railshosting.org](http://www.railshosting.org/) has made a nice deal with Dreamhost, using the promocode 50OFFRAILS you get 50$ off on any hostig plan. I got another 20% discount by ordering 2 years of hosting up front. The result is about 140$, or 5.83$ a month. Not bad I'd say.
