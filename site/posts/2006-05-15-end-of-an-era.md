_This was published on a personal blog I ran during the nillies, it's included here as a historical archive. Links and images may be broken, it's been a while._

The last week of class for me, ever, has begun. In four more days it will be over. My life as a student is nearing completion. I loved every moment of it, but I'll be glad when it's done. By the end of June my exams will be finished and I'll be off to Ireland for the annual [European Juggling Convetion](http://www.ejc2006.net/). Imagine a few thousand jugglers and related artists together for over a week. It's _crazy_ , believe me. The first time I went to the EJC it was in Rotterdam. That must have been 2001 I guess. Haven't missed a single one since then. The Netherlands, Germany, Denmark, France, Slovenia and this year in Dublin, Ireland. It might be the last one for a while, since I'll most probably be on the other side of the globe by then, so best believe I'm going to enjoy this one. 

But first finish the last courses on my curriculum. Some computer science, some mandarin chinese, and then once I get back from Ireland I'll have 'till the end of august to finish my thesis. Aight! I've been experimenting with some patterns from NLPweekly, such as [this one](http://www.nlpweekly.com/?p=42) to quickly learn a foreign language. Since Chinese is a bit different from other languages, it needs some alteration, still it seems to be working quite well. 

_Update_ : This years juggling convention is **not** happening in the Dublin area as I tought, in fact it's in Millstreet, almost 300km from Dublin. The same town where Belgium became last on the Eurovision Song Festival back in 1993. (Belgium : 3 points :))
